ZOI Shape Presets
  • This page allows you to manage the available E-test ZOI shape presets. This is required, as the shape is highly dependent on the antibiotic or other conditions. You can add new items into the list or import/export presets from/to a file. If you remove a shape preset, data that was configured with that preset continue to work.
